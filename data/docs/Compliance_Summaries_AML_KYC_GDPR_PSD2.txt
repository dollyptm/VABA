# VABA - Compliance Summaries (Training)
These are simplified summaries for educational use.

AML / KYC
- KYC verifies identity via document checks
- AML monitors for suspicious activity and enforces thresholds and reviews
- Risk-based approach: higher-risk activity may require additional checks

GDPR (EU Privacy)
- Lawful bases for processing; minimize data collection
- Rights: access, rectification, erasure (production contexts)
- Retention limits; delete data when no longer needed

PSD2 (EU Payments)
- SCA: Strong Customer Authentication principles
- Third-party access allowed with user consent and secure APIs
- Clear audit trails and revocation mechanisms

Banko should not give final legal approvals; escalate to compliance for official decisions.
