# VABA - Internal Compliance Memos (Training)
High-level guidance for training.

Acceptable Use
- No illegal content or activity
- No real PII or secrets in the training environment

Prohibited Transactions
- Transactions designed to evade monitoring or sanctions
- Attempts to simulate real-world laundering with real accounts

Monitoring Thresholds (Examples)
- Large transaction triggers (e.g., >= $10,000)
- Rapid multiple transfers across accounts
- Unusual patterns for the user profile

Record-Keeping & Escalation
- Keep transaction metadata
- Escalate ambiguous or risky cases to a trainer or designated reviewer
