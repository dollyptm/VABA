# VABA - Rewards FAQ

Q: How many points per dollar?
A: 1 point per $1 on eligible purchases ('purchase', 'benefit_purchase').

Q: Are there category bonuses?
A: Training mode may include examples: groceries +5%, travel +10%, dining +8%.

Q: Can I transfer points to other users?
A: Not supported in this training build.

Q: Do points expire?
A: In training, assume no expiry unless stated for your session.

Q: Where can I see my points?
A: Check the Benefits page or ask Banko at /banko.

Q: How do I redeem?
A: Choose a tier on Benefits and confirm; system records a loyalty_redemption transaction. Banko will not auto-redeem without your confirmation.
