# VABA - Rewards Program Rules
VABA’s rewards are for training. Banko can explain rules and summarize points; redemption requires your confirmation in the UI.

Earning Points
- Base accrual: 1 point per $1 on transactions with transaction_type in ('purchase','benefit_purchase')
- Computed from the transactions table for a user’s accounts
- Optional category bonuses (examples): groceries +5%, travel +10%, dining +8%

Redemption Tiers (examples)
- Cashback $5 -> 500 points
- Cashback $10 -> 950 points
- Cashback $25 -> 2250 points
- Account Bonus $15 -> 1200 points
- Account Bonus $50 -> 3800 points
- Premium Upgrade Credit -> 1500 points
- Shopping Voucher $30 -> 2800 points

Redemption Flow
1) Choose a tier on the Benefits page
2) Confirm selection
3) System records a loyalty_redemption transaction (points deduction may be simulated in training)

Ask Banko
- "How many points do I have?"
- "Am I eligible for $10 cashback?"
- "How are points calculated?"

Figures are for training and may vary per session.
