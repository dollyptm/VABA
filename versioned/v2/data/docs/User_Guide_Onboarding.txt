# VABA - User Guide & Onboarding
Welcome to VABA (Vulnerable AI Banking Application). Inside VABA, your AI assistant is Banko.

This app is for education only. Do not use real personal data.

Getting Started
1) Open an account
   - Eligibility: adults with a valid email/phone
   - Required info: name, email, phone, address, date of birth
   - KYC: upload a clear image/PDF of your ID (Profile -> KYC)
   - Review time: training-only; status may update quickly

2) Login / Logout
   - Log in with the credentials you registered with
   - Use the top navigation to log out when finished

3) Profile Management
   - Update bio, contact info, preferences (language, time zone, notifications)
   - KYC upload at Profile -> KYC

4) Viewing Balances & Transactions
   - Dashboard/Home: balances, recent transactions, summaries
   - Statements: download or review summaries

5) Transfers
   - Transfers page to move funds between your accounts
   - Shows applicable limits/fees and processing estimate

6) Using Banko (Chatbot)
   - Go to /banko to chat with Banko, your VABA assistant
   - Ask about balances, transactions, rewards rules, or app usage
   - Banko can also use uploaded docs (/docs) and summarize DB data when appropriate

7) Docs Center
   - Upload PDFs/TXTs at /docs, then click Rebuild Index so Banko can use them

Tips
- Keep questions short and specific
- Rewards: ask "How many points do I have?" or "Am I eligible for $10 cashback?"
- KYC: ask "What is KYC?" or "Where do I upload my ID?"

This guide reflects the training build of VABA and may differ from production systems.
