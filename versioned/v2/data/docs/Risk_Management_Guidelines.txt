# VABA - Risk Management Guidelines (Training)

Authentication & Sessions
- Use strong passwords in real systems
- Session timeouts and CSRF protections are expected in production

Change Management
- Code changes should be reviewed and tested in staging
- Track changes and approvals

Incident Response
- Define severity levels and SLAs (training context)
- Notify users in case of material data exposure in real deployments

Backups & Recovery
- Back up databases regularly
- Test restoration procedures
