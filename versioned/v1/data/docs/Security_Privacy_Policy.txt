# VABA - Security & Privacy (Training)
This outlines how the training build handles data. Not legal advice.

Data Handling
- Personal data: name, email, phone, address, DOB, preferences, limited KYC metadata
- Storage: CockroachDB (bank schema: users, accounts, transactions, benefits); KYC files on disk for demo
- Passwords: stored as hashes; sessions: cookie-based
- LLM usage: Banko may use de-identified context; minimize sensitive content

Privacy
- Purpose: hands-on education
- Retention: datasets may reset; do not upload real personal data
- Rights: for production, rights include access/rectification/erasure; in training, contact instructor

Operational Security (High-Level)
- Separate configs for dev vs prod recommended
- Periodic backups for resets
- Avoid real secrets in training environments
